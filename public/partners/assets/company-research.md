# Partner Outreach Research

Research compiled May 2026 from each company's website, blog, Instagram caption excerpts (visible via embedded feeds), and award listings. Where a specific claim could not be verified publicly (e.g. private builder partnerships), this is noted explicitly so we don't bluff in outreach.

## 1. Dream Doors Kitchens Sunshine Coast — @dreamdoorskitchensaus

**Owner/franchisee:** Glenn Stanton (Sunshine Coast & Moreton Bay territory)
**Showroom:** Maroochydore, QLD
**Instagram handle:** @dreamdoorskitchensaus is the **national** Dream Doors Australia account (~1,611 followers, 577 posts). Local Sunshine Coast content runs primarily through the Facebook page (facebook.com/dreamdoorssunshinecoast) and the location landing page on dreamdoorskitchens.com.au.

### What they actually do
DDK is a **retail kitchen-renovation franchise**, not a builder. Their core product is the "kitchen facelift" — re-using existing cabinet carcasses and swapping doors, handles, benchtops and splashbacks. They also do full new kitchens. Glenn's team markets a "3-day kitchen reveal" as their headline promise. They are not a B2B/builder cabinetry shop, so framing should be homeowner-renovation-led, not volume-builder-led.

### Recent / current activity (last ~6 months)
- The location page was updated 6 Feb 2026 (Glenn's bio, suburb coverage, supplier list refreshed).
- DDK nationally has an entire page dedicated to **engineered stone legislation / silica information** — they are visibly navigating the post-2024 engineered-stone ban and are leaning on Caesarstone's new low/no-silica products plus laminate alternatives.
- Featured supplier badges on the Sunshine Coast page: **Caesarstone, Polytec, Corian Design, Tesrol, Lincoln Sentry, Nover, Häfele, Blum**.
- "Australian Made" is front-and-centre in their 2026 creative.
- 10-year warranty on locally-sourced doors, cabinetry and laminate benchtops.

### Builder partnerships
None named publicly. The business model is direct-to-homeowner via the local showroom, with referrals from past clients. **Do not claim a specific builder partnership in outreach — there's no public source for one.**

### Service area highlights
Caloundra, Mooloolaba, Maroochydore, Buderim, Noosa Heads, Coolum Beach, Caboolture, Bribie Island, Sippy Downs, Pelican Waters, Baringa.

### Personalisation hooks
1. **The engineered-stone pivot.** "I noticed Dream Doors has been very upfront with homeowners about the engineered-stone changes — there's a dedicated info page on the national site. Alpha Surfaces was built specifically as a low-silica alternative that drops in where Caesarstone used to sit, with the same fabricator workflow your installers already know."
2. **The 3-day facelift promise.** "Your 3-day reveal turnaround is one of the tightest in the renovation market. Our [product] ships pre-templated within [X days] from order, which means Glenn's team can hit that 3-day window without the benchtop being the bottleneck."
3. **Australian-made positioning.** "The Australian-Made badge on the Sunshine Coast page is doing real work in your creative — if Alpha is also Australian-fabricated, lead with that as common ground. Caesarstone and Polytec are already on your supplier wall; we'd like to be next to them."

## 3. Dimension Stone — @dimension_stone

**Locations:**
- **Victoria HQ:** Unit 1, 28–30 Little Boundary Rd, Laverton North VIC
- **Queensland:** 36 Ereton Drive, Arundel QLD 4214 (Gold Coast)

**Instagram followers:** ~1,613 (figure user supplied — Instagram does not surface a public count via search, treat as roughly correct).

### What they actually do
Stone benchtop fabrication & installation. They are a **fabricator/installer**, not a stone wholesaler — they supply, manufacture and install. They serve **homeowners, builders, and cabinet makers** across VIC and QLD. They claim 5,000+ clients and 8,000+ projects to date.

### Recent / current activity (last ~6 months)
- **Website was rebuilt on Webflow recently** — domain footer reads "© 2026 Dimension Stone" and the design is a clear refresh vs the older Google-listed site. New booking flow, new HubSpot meeting links (separate ones for VIC and QLD), refreshed quote process page.
- **Dual-state operation is now front-and-centre** — every page has both phone numbers and addresses. The QLD Arundel showroom is being actively pushed, suggesting recent or ongoing expansion into south-east QLD.
- **WorkSafe Victoria Approved** badge is displayed prominently — a deliberate trust signal post-engineered-stone ban.
- Their blog cadence is sparse but **the direction is clear**: May 2025 *Natural Stone vs Porcelain*, June 2025 *How to Choose the Perfect Benchtop*, **Oct 2025 *Why Porcelain Benchtops Are the Future of Kitchen Design***. They are repositioning around **porcelain** (Dekton, Neolith) as the post-engineered-stone hero product.

### Brands they currently stock / partner with
Caesarstone, Stone Ambassador, CDK Stone, Cosentino (Silestone + Dekton), Lavi Stone, AC Stone, Surface Spectrum, WK Stone, Neolith, Vadara, Smartstone. That's the full premium spectrum — natural, engineered and porcelain.

### Trade/builder signals
Their testimonials include **"Ortiguerra Manalansan Architects"** (architecture practice) and a customer named "Bret" who says: "I've had the team at Dimension Stone on a number of projects ranging from single dwellings to **high end townhouse developments**." So they do work for developers and architects, even if no individual builder is named on the homepage.

### Personalisation hooks
1. **The porcelain pivot.** "Your Oct 2025 blog 'Why Porcelain Benchtops Are the Future' read like a manifesto — most fabricators are still hedging. Alpha's [porcelain/UV-stable] range would slot directly into the Dekton/Neolith shelf you already promote, with [X advantage]."
2. **The new Arundel showroom.** "I noticed the Gold Coast showroom at 36 Ereton Drive is being pushed as hard as Laverton — congrats on the QLD expansion. We can supply Arundel direct without a Melbourne stopover, which knocks freight off your QLD jobs."
3. **WorkSafe-approved post-ban positioning.** "Your WorkSafe Victoria Approved badge is doing real lifting in builder/specifier confidence — we're a [low-silica / silica-free] product designed for fabricators in exactly your situation, so we don't undo the trust you've built post-July-2024."

## Cross-company observations for the outreach campaign

- **Three of the four are post-engineered-stone-ban sensitive** (DDK has a dedicated info page, Dimension Stone leads with WorkSafe-approved + porcelain pivot, Impala specs marble + Caesarstone's new ranges). Lead every email with the silica/safety angle.
- **Only Hammertime is a true volume builder cabinetry shop.** DDK and Impala are direct-to-homeowner; Dimension Stone is a fabricator that serves both channels. Tailor the value prop accordingly — Hammertime gets the multi-res spec story, the others get the design-led story.
- **Three are QLD/Sunshine-Coast region** (DDK Sunshine Coast, Dimension Stone Arundel, Hammertime Baringa). If we're shipping out of a QLD or south-east-QLD warehouse, mention same-state lead times in each email. Impala is Sydney metro only.
- **Award credibility carries the most weight with Impala**; **volume / install speed with Hammertime**; **post-ban material innovation with Dimension Stone**; **fabricator workflow + 3-day promise with DDK**.
