Why Alpha, specifically

Four reasons we are worth a conversation.

01

Zero crystalline silica

Engineered stone has been banned in Australia since July 2024. Our surfaces contain zero crystalline silica, so your fabricators and installers are not working around the problem the ban was written for.

02

AlphaShield™ warranty

A written, lifetime stain resistance guarantee — fewer callbacks in the first two years after handover, on the surface your client touches every single day.

03

Jumbo slabs, fewer joins

3200 × 1600 × 20mm sheets mean fewer joins on an island bench and a faster install — which matters most on the jobs where the kitchen is the photograph.

04

UV-stable outdoor range

Genuine UV-stable colours built for the Queensland climate — outdoor kitchens and alfresco benches that hold their colour through a Mackay summer.
