Delivered since the last meeting

↑ Agenda

Sample dispatch — nearly done

  • Jess’s account confirmed as MyPost Business, not eParcel. That one answer decided the whole approach.
  • The bridge is built and tested: Pipedrive lead → address resolved → Australia Post label created → tracking number written back to Pipedrive.
  • A sample is only flagged sent when Australia Post physically scans the parcel — not when the label prints. A printed label sitting on a shelf will never show as sent.
  • Every label print charges the card immediately, so there is a hard double-charge guard: the order is recorded before printing, and recovery re-fetches the existing label rather than buying a second one.
  • Jess stops typing addresses by hand. Twelve to fifteen a day.

Lead stages — rebuilt and live

  • Five stages that each say whose turn it is, replacing a list that mixed stages, actions and blocked reasons.
  • Lead outcome added — Converted, Referred to stonemason, Nurture, Unreachable, Not a prospect, Duplicate, Junk. Referred to stonemason is the win condition for influencer leads, which had no way of being counted as a win before.
  • Done by renaming in place, so no lead had to be migrated and nothing was stranded.
  • First training with Jess is Thursday.

Fixed along the way

  • A serious security hole. Every RoadRunner API endpoint was serving data to anonymous callers from the open internet, with owner privileges — prospect emails included. Found, closed, verified.
  • Email open and click tracking was never connected at SendGrid’s end. Now live, so every future campaign has full data.
  • Partner pages were caching for four hours, so corrections did not appear. Now sixty seconds.
  • Password reset links now last seven days instead of two hours.
  • A campaign engagement report added to the RoadRunner dashboard.

Datapel Breeze — the $100 question

↑ Agenda

Jana asked it plainly: “We will be moving to Odoo in November — is it a point, or do the girls need it?”

Dominic has confirmed the Breeze API is not enabled on the licence. Enabling it is $100 per month ex GST. He will create the API user and whitelist our addresses; the endpoint is live and documented.

What we would actually get

  • Customers, price levels, products, sales orders and lines, inventory quantities, sales reps.
  • In plain terms: who is genuinely buying and how much — enough to grade accounts on real numbers instead of impressions, and to build the Top 50 properly.
  • But not samples. Yana and Cherie removed every sample from Datapel on the accountant’s advice about inventory value. The sample stock-out case — a large part of why we wanted Datapel — has no data in Datapel any more, and is back to a manual spreadsheet.

The recommendation — and what it hangs on

  • If November holds: don’t pay it. That is roughly $300 for a connection we then throw away, and Odoo’s API is included rather than a monthly add-on.
  • The valuable output is a one-off matching exercise, not a live feed. Sophie already exports Datapel to Excel for slab counts. One export gets us account grading and the Top 50 now, for nothing.
  • If Odoo slips to next year: pay it. That would be six months or more with no live data, and $600 is cheap for that visibility. A working sync would also prove the pattern before Odoo, when the stakes are higher.
  • Either way the work is not wasted — the shape of the data does not change, only the source.
  • So the real question for the room is whether November actually holds.

Worth revisiting separately: crediting inventory and debiting a marketing expense account would have kept the samples visible at zero value. That is ordinary accounting, and the current answer is a spreadsheet nobody can see.

What “sent” should mean

↑ Agenda

Two signals come back from Australia Post, and they mean different things. The build assumes the stricter one — confirm that is right.

SignalWhenWhat it actually proves
Tracking number issued Immediately, on the label call A label exists and the card has been charged. The box may still be on a shelf. Not sent
Courier scan When Australia Post physically takes it They have the parcel. This is what writes the Samples sent date. Sent

This is the difference between a report that says what we posted and a report that says what we printed. Worth being deliberate about it now, because every follow-up sequence counts days from that date.
