eParcel. Alpha sends roughly 3,000–3,750 parcels a year against Australia Post’s 2,000 threshold. eParcel brings negotiated rates, scheduled pickup from the warehouse, and the one thing MyPost Business can’t do: confirmation that Australia Post actually has the parcel. The application is a callback form on the Australia Post site, about three minutes, and the answers are prepared. To decide: whose name goes on it — Sean, so it moves today, or Jess on hello@, who runs it day to day.
