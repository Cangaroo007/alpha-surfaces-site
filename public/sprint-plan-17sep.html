F · Trip planning and the road-trip view

Back to the plan

F1 — the planner. Live since 31 August; booking a stop writes a real CRM activity.

F2 — the road-trip view. Back on the list. A read-only forward look at who is visiting whom, where, next week, with enough beside each row to judge whether the day is worth joining.

Pipedrive cannot do it: the schedule lives on the activity and the account context lives on the organisation, and no Pipedrive view joins the two. That join is the build — about half a day.

What both need is the same thing: rep territory definitions and postcodes. That is the ask, not the code.
