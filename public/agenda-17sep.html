It is safe to practise on right now. The carrier is set to plain label, so pressing dispatch produces a label but books nothing with Australia Post and charges nothing. That is deliberate: the best week to break it is the week it cannot cost anything.

Every new sample request now carries a link straight to its own dispatch page in the Pipedrive note, so Jess starts where she already works.

Three things happen on their own once she presses it: the lead moves to Following up, the tracking number writes itself back, and Belinda gets an email on the requests worth telling her about. Samples sent fills in when Australia Post scans the parcel — which is the definition we agreed: a printed label is not a sent sample.

The one decision

  • When to turn the live carrier on. The moment it flips, every dispatch buys a real label and charges the MyPost Business card. The sensible order is: Jess uses it in plain-label mode until she is bored of it, then we flip and buy one deliberate label on an address we can verify.
  • eParcel — Alpha comfortably qualifies on volume. It is free, removes the $126/month Starshipit fee and gives real lodgement confirmation. Worth Jess starting the application.

4 · Two Pipedrive settings to make

Back to agenda

Both were agreed on Monday. Both are settings changes rather than builds, and both need someone with administrator rights in Pipedrive.

Sample drop activity type

  • Two custom types already exist, so there is no plan limit in the way.
  • It is what makes the six-a-day measurable for the first time — a two-minute pop-in and a booked hour stop being the same record.
  • ⚠️ Change the coverage report in the same sitting. That report counts contact as call, meeting, lunch, showroom visit. Add the type without adding it to that list and sample drops quietly stop counting as contact.

The six role labels

  • Agreed on Monday: rather than chase job titles nobody uses, pick a rough role from a short list — Owner/Director, Manager, Estimator, Designer/Specifier, Sales/Showroom, Admin/Accounts.
  • A free-text job title field stays available for when someone hands over a card that has one, as Olivia pointed out.
  • The current Labels field holds 23 options doing three different jobs, so the six go in as their own thing rather than on top.

Both are still open, and both are blocked on the same thing as Olivia’s admin session — administrator access. Worth settling that today; it unblocks three separate items.

6 · The road-trip view

Back to agenda

Belinda asked for this in August: “If I want to jump in the car with someone for a day, I can’t see who they’re seeing.” It is back on the sprint list, with its own definition.

A read-only forward look — who is visiting whom, where, next week — with enough beside each row (grade, category, slabs a month, last visit) to judge whether a day is worth joining. The trip planner already books the stops; this is the view over the top of them.

It is about half a day of build. The thing that makes it worth building is rep territory definitions and postcodes — without those the geography is guesswork, and geography is the whole point. That is the ask, not the code.
