1 · Which field — Business Category, not Label

Back to agenda

Two people can filter for the same thing in Pipedrive and get two different numbers, and both are reading the system correctly. There are two fields that both answer “what kind of business is this”, and they do not hold the same values.

Of 3,156 organisations
2,637have both fields setand 2,432 of those agree
448have a Label but no Business Categoryinvisible to any category filter
14have a Business Category but no Labelthe small side of the gap
57have neitherthe clean-up list

Stonemasons read 336 by Business Category and 344 by Label. Cabinet makers read 1,279 and 1,630, because Label also carries Kitchen Retail as a separate value. Neither number is wrong. They are answers to two different questions.

5 · Trip planner — Pam used it in anger

Back to agenda

Pam planned a real multi-town run with it and said it was easier than booking three towns by hand. That is the useful signal. Everything below is what she found while doing it.

Before the training

  • Bookings landed in Jess’s calendar. Pam reassigned them by hand. The write is going through the wrong key rather than the signed-in rep’s. This one first
  • Company names stopped populating the activity header between the first run and the second. Same code, different result, so it needs finding rather than patching

Pam’s list, in her order

  • A time selector on Book — everything lands at the top of the day
  • Split Booked from Confirmed — she books a day, then rings
  • Multi-select on categories and grades — exclude builders, or take A and B
  • Save a trip as a reusable list, re-datable — this is the call cycle
  • Print run sheet: add the phone number and the sample status

Pam’s own words on the rest were “chip away bit by bit”, which is the right instruction. The two at the top are the ones that matter before the training, on either date.
