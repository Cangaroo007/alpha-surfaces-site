B · Form rebuild One day

Back to the plan

The root cause: Unit / House No. is a required field, and most people don’t have one.

Unit boxStreet boxWhat they meant
99 Sunnymede Lane9 Sunnymede Lane
94Ring Street94 Ring Street
211 Sundew St2/11 Sundew St
1-9, 8Mulligan Stnobody knows

Requiring an optional-by-nature field doesn’t produce more data. It produces noise that looks like data. Roughly half of all records needed unit-and-street reconciliation before they could be used.

The fix — about a day. One address line instead of two. Postcode first, auto-filling suburb and state from the free Australia Post dataset (~16,000 rows, a static JSON file, no API key and no runtime dependency). A “Sending to:” confirmation line before submit. The 2 September spec already specified one address line — the build diverged. This is finishing something already decided.

One form change moves four sprints.

M gets the clean structured addresses Australia Post needs. J grows on its own, because cabinet makers and builders name their stonemason on the way in. N gets architects self-identifying for CPD. A gets project stage, which is something the sequences can branch on.

A · Lead capture & automations Week five

Back to the plan

Lead stages were reworked on 6 September — New · Working · Following up · Qualified · Closed, plus a Lead outcome field. Renamed in place, so nothing needed migrating and it stays reversible.

93 of 101 leads sat on “New”, and every other stage held zero.

Nobody has ever moved a lead off New. That changes what training has to say — not “here are the new stage names” but “the stage is how everyone else knows whose turn it is, and moving it is part of the job.”

Structural point worth keeping: for an influencer lead, the deal is not with the person who enquired. A homeowner lead has no “won” state of its own — its best outcome is Referred to stonemason, or every specifier lead looks like a loss.

Blocked on Belinda’s voice recording, now into week five. All four automations and every follow-up sequence wait on it. Two routing decisions are still open: do stonemason sample requests go straight to the owning rep or through Jess first, and do cabinet makers and builders get a rep call or a nurture sequence?

E · Datapel & the Top 50 Blocked

Back to the plan

Approved at $100/month on 8 September. Signup is still open, waiting on the login.

The integration is designed — OData mirror, matching table, phases P0–P4 — and the discovery scripts run the day credentials land. It is the biggest single dependency in the engagement: the Top 50, sales by customer, the buying-versus-visited quadrant and Olivia’s price level field all sit behind it. Odoo has moved to January, so this is six-plus months of visibility for $600.

Two traps recorded in the design, worth not rediscovering.

RoadRunner runs in Railway’s Virginia region; Datapel is Australian-hosted and may be IP-restricted, and Railway doesn’t hand out static egress IPs by default. And changing Railway region changes the egress IPs — so if allowlisting is needed, move region first and allowlist after. The other order breaks the sync weeks later with a firewall error nobody connects back to the region change.

Also open: whether the invoice of record is Datapel or MYOB AccountRight — that one question could redirect the whole sprint. Smartstone reports asked 3×. The pool business decision asked 5×.
