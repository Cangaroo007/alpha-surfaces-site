Questions

What is AlphaShield™?
AlphaShield™ is our exclusive surface protection, applied during manufacture and embedded in the top layer of the surface. It resists stains from red wine, coffee and acidic foods, and most household chemicals, and is backed by a Lifetime Stain Resistance Guarantee.
Are Alpha Surfaces silica-free?
Yes. Alpha Surfaces are made with a zero silica formulation from premium minerals and recycled materials such as glass.
How do I order a sample?
Pick up to three surfaces in the collection on this page and press Order free samples. Samples and delivery are free.
Where can I see Alpha Surfaces in person?
At our Sunshine Coast showroom in Kunda Park, our Gold Coast showroom inside the De Dietrich Appliance Centre in Labrador, or through one of our stonemason partners. See showroom details.
Does AlphaShield™ wear off over time?
No. AlphaShield™ is applied in the final stage of manufacture and embedded in the top layer of the surface, filling its microscopic pores. It is invisible and permanent — there is nothing to reapply or reseal.
What stains does AlphaShield™ protect against?
Everyday kitchen stains such as red wine, coffee, oil and acidic foods, as well as most household chemicals. It also has built-in microbial protection.
What warranty is provided?
A 15-Year Limited Warranty covering manufacturing defects, plus the AlphaShield™ Lifetime Stain Resistance Guarantee. Full warranty terms.
How many designs are available?
30+ current designs across five collections, from soft whites and warm neutrals to bold veining and deep tones.
Will my benchtop look exactly like the sample?
A sample shows you the colour, finish and character of a surface. Like natural stone, a full slab has movement and variation a small sample can’t fully show — especially in veined designs — so we recommend seeing a full slab before you finalise your choice.
Can I use Alpha Surfaces outdoors?
Yes — the four designs in our Indoor-Outdoor range (Whitehaven, Cabarita, Torquay and Broome) are UV-stable and suited to exterior use. We recommend covered outdoor areas for the best long-term performance.
