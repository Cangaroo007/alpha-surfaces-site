7%

Only seven per cent of completed activities carry a person. Five per cent carry both a person and a company — 199 records out of 3,973.

The team logged 3,973 activities in six months, about sixteen a day. The work is happening; the CRM cannot see who it was with. A six per cent rate identical across meetings and calls is a workflow default, not effort — it is what happens when you log from the organisation record instead of the contact.

Also corrected: 485 activities open and never closed, not 293. And showroom check-in is inverted — 67 walk-ins, every one recording a person and no business.

Training program — week of 8 September

↑ Agenda

The three non-negotiables. If the team does nothing else, these fix most of what the audit found. Everything else is optional by comparison.

  1. Every activity carries a company AND a person. No company, it didn't happen. No person, we can never contact them again. Two taps — and it fixes the 7%.
  2. Tick it done, and pick the right type — and the type must say whether it was a formal meeting or a sample drop. Fixes the 485 open activities and makes the six-a-day KPI measurable.
  3. Never leave a visit without booking the next one. Turns the CRM into a call cycle instead of a logbook.

Start from the contact record, not the organisation. That one change in where you begin is the whole fix — no extra field to remember.
