The awards campaign is built

  • Pam’s feedback is in and processed. She has reviewed the award spreadsheet, sorted it, and marked every builder she has not yet contacted.
  • The send list is a live Google Sheet — 53 companies, each with its award count, region, categories won, and a current CRM status. Empty columns for contact name, email, phone and website, and a Send? column so Pam controls exactly who goes.
  • The EDM is written to Pam’s brief, in her order: congratulate them on the win, introduce Alpha, the features, benefits and five collections, Pam as their named point of contact, and it closes by asking for a short introductory meeting. It sends from her address with replies coming back to her.

  • Two guards worth knowing: a builder with no email address is reported and skipped, never quietly dropped; and the system refuses to send any message that still has an unanswered question in it. The three open copy decisions below are therefore impossible to send by accident.

Why the swatches were blank

↑ Contents

Olivia flagged Viola, Taj Mahal and Satchari Gold rendering blank in the awards EDM. It is not broken links, and it is not those three colours.

Every stone image on the site is a WebP

  • 211 image files, and not one JPEG or PNG fallback anywhere. Outlook on Windows does not render WebP. Any swatch dropped into an email is a blank white box for a large share of builders — it would have hit all thirty-seven current colours, not the three that happened to get noticed.
  • Two of the three are not products. There is no “Viola” — the real colours are Calacatta Viola and Viola Ligera. There is no “Satchari Gold” — most likely Statuario Gold, and the Collection 05 gold is Autumn Gold.
  • Taj Mahal does exist and its files are all present, which is exactly what you would expect if the format is at fault rather than the asset.
  • So the EDM carries no images at all. The five collections are set in type; the swatches live on the landing page, where WebP is fine. The Fergus email made the same choice and it was the right one. If swatches in the email are wanted later, the fix is a small JPEG set generated for email only.
