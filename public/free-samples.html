Questions

How many samples can I order?
Up to three per order. Tick your favourites in the grid above and they’ll be ready on the order form.
Are samples and delivery both free?
Yes. The samples are free and so is delivery to any Australian street address. We can’t deliver to PO boxes.
When will my sample arrive?
We usually post samples within two business days of your order, by Australia Post. Please allow up to two weeks for delivery, depending on where you are.
What is included in a sample order?
A small piece of each surface you chose (up to three), so you can see the colour, finish and pattern in your own light and next to your cabinetry, tiles and paint.
Will my benchtop look exactly like my sample?
A sample shows you the colour, finish and character of a surface. Like natural stone, a full slab has movement and variation that a small sample can’t fully show — especially in veined designs — so we recommend seeing a full slab before you finalise your choice.
What is AlphaShield™?
AlphaShield™ is our exclusive surface protection, applied during manufacture and embedded in the top layer of the surface. It resists stains from red wine, coffee and acidic foods, and most household chemicals, and is backed by a Lifetime Stain Resistance Guarantee.
Are Alpha Surfaces silica-free?
Yes. Alpha Surfaces are made with a zero silica formulation from premium minerals and recycled materials such as glass. The safety data sheet is on our About page.

How do I buy Alpha Surfaces?
Alpha Surfaces are supplied and installed through our network of authorised stonemasons and distributors. Your kitchen company or stonemason can quote on any design, or call us on 1300 257 420 and we’ll connect you.
What warranty is provided?
Alpha Surfaces carry a 15-Year Limited Warranty covering manufacturing defects, and AlphaShield™ adds a Lifetime Stain Resistance Guarantee. Full warranty terms.

Why Alpha Surfaces

Zero silica.
Alpha Surfaces are made with a zero silica formulation from premium minerals and recycled materials such as glass. Australia has banned engineered stone containing crystalline silica because the dust from cutting it harms the people who make benchtops. Ours contains none.
Engineered for fabrication safety.
With no crystalline silica, cutting and polishing an Alpha surface doesn’t produce silica dust. That makes it safer for the stonemasons who fabricate and install your benchtop.
AlphaShield™ — Lifetime Stain Resistance Guarantee.
Our exclusive surface protection is applied during manufacture and embedded in the top layer of the surface. It resists everyday stains such as red wine, coffee, oil and acidic foods, never needs resealing, and is backed for life.
15-Year Limited Warranty.
Alpha Surfaces carry a 15-Year Limited Warranty covering manufacturing defects. Full warranty terms.
NSF & Greenguard certified.
Independently tested: NSF certification for safe food contact, and UL Greenguard and Greenguard Gold certification for low chemical emissions and healthier indoor air.
