Questions

What is AlphaShield™?
AlphaShield™ is our exclusive surface protection, applied during manufacture and embedded in the top layer of the surface. It resists stains from red wine, coffee and acidic foods, and most household chemicals, and is backed by a Lifetime Stain Resistance Guarantee.
Are Alpha Surfaces silica-free?
Yes. Alpha Surfaces are made with a zero silica formulation from premium minerals and recycled materials such as glass. The safety data sheet is on our About page.
Will my benchtop look exactly like my sample?
A sample shows you the colour, finish and character of a surface. Like natural stone, a full slab has movement and variation that a small sample can’t fully show — especially in veined designs — so we recommend seeing a full slab before you finalise your choice.
How much do samples cost?
Nothing. Samples are free, and so is delivery.
