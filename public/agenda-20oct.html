3 · Sample drops and QR scanning

Back to agenda

One QR code per sample type. Reps scan twice in RoadRunner, and RoadRunner does the Pipedrive side for them.

StepWhat happens
1 · Pick upScan each sample when taking it from Kunda Park or Prestons. It goes onto that rep’s stock.
2 · DropScan at the company. RoadRunner logs a completed Sample drop in Pipedrive with what was left and who took it, and moves it from the rep’s stock to the company.
HistoryEvery drop stays on record, so each company has a full history of what it has been given. Nothing is overwritten.
ChecksBoth scans check the phone is on site. Drops entered by hand are marked as such, so we can see where scanning isn’t being used.
StockAt any time: what’s at each office, what each rep is carrying, and what each company holds.

First step: Belinda’s list of every sample item and version. Each one gets its own QR code. The 450 new boxes get clear stickers.

4 · Better date ranges and comparisons, everywhere

Back to agenda

Choosing a date range is clumsy today, and the Forms dashboard is the worst of it. One filter bar is being built for every RoadRunner report and dashboard:

  • Quick ranges: this week, last week, this month, last month, this quarter, financial year to date, last 12 months
  • Any custom range with a calendar
  • Compare with the previous period, or the same period last year, with the change shown on every number
  • Filters for rep, state and business type that stay set as you move between pages, and links that open with the same filters

Forms first, then the sales reports, then the KPI dashboard.
